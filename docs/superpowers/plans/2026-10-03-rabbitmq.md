# RabbitMQ implementation plan

**Goal:** Complete issue #1 slice 5 with managed RabbitMQ channels and compose the infrastructure lifecycle.

**Architecture:** One connection with separate declaration, confirm-publisher, and
manual-ack consumer channels. Prepared publications keep immutable bytes/IDs/expiry.
Bound publisher confirmations and consumer work by prefetch. Latch broker/channel
failures; the lifecycle owns recovery. No driver replay of uncertain publications.

**Stack:** lapin/Tokio, existing contracts, isolated local RabbitMQ integration tests.
